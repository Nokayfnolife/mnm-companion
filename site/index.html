Monsters and Memories Companion

A leveling planner for quests, gear and zone maps

Sign in to start

Set your level and see what to do next, the gear worth chasing, and where it all is on the map. It's free, and your characters follow you from PC to phone.

or

We'll send a one-time sign-in link. No password needed.

The characters saved in this browser move into your account the first time you sign in.

Guided or Classic

Guided lays out quests, hunting spots and armor sets for your level. Classic is a plain character sheet, with upgrade tips only if you ask for them.

Your gear, your stats

Pick what you're wearing in each slot. Your stats add up on their own, and only the items that beat your gear show as upgrades.

Zone maps

Community maps with pins for camps, quest givers and turn-ins, plus notes you drop yourself.

Search and ask

Find any item, quest or zone as you type, or ask a question and get an answer drawn from the wiki.
